Aesthetics & Organization

Minimalist Bullet Systems

Bullet journaling was originally designed as a rapid, analog system to track the past, organize the present, and plan for the future. However, social media has turned it into an intimidating art contest. It is time to strip away the overwhelming artistic pressure and return to the core methodology.

The Return to Rapid Logging

True organization does not require watercolor borders. It requires a system that is faster to use than trying to hold a thought in your working memory. Adopt a strict rapid logging approach using a simple key.
